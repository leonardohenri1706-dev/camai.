### **1. Executive Summary**

*   **What is this project about?** (High-level overview of the product/feature and its purpose)
*   **Why are we doing this?** (Brief statement of the core problem it solves and the value it delivers)
*   **What is the desired outcome?** (A quick look at the main goal)

---

### **2. Problem Statement & Opportunity**

*   **What problem are we solving?**
    *   Describe the specific pain point, challenge, or unmet need for our users/customers.
    *   Quantify the problem if possible (e.g., "users spend X minutes on Y task," "Z% churn due to lack of W").
*   **For whom are we solving this problem?** (Specific user segment affected)
*   **Why is this a problem worth solving now?** (Urgency, market trend, competitive pressure, strategic alignment)
*   **What is the market opportunity?** (If applicable, describe the potential growth, revenue, or strategic advantage)

---

### **3. Goals & Objectives**

*   **What are we trying to achieve with this project?** (SMART: Specific, Measurable, Achievable, Relevant, Time-bound)
    *   *Example:* Increase user engagement by 15% within Q3 by enabling X feature.
    *   *Example:* Reduce customer support tickets related to Y by 25% within 6 weeks.
    *   *Example:* Launch MVP by [Date] to validate [Hypothesis].
*   **How will we measure success?** (Key Performance Indicators - KPIs)
    *   List specific metrics that will indicate whether the objectives have been met.

---

### **4. Target Audience / Users**

*   **Who are the primary users of this product/feature?** (e.g., existing customers, new users, specific persona like "Small Business Owner Sarah")
*   **What are their core needs, behaviors, and pain points relevant to this project?**

---

### **5. Proposed Solution & Key Features**

*   **High-Level Solution Overview:** Describe the proposed product or feature set that addresses the problem.
*   **Key Features / Functionality:**
    *   List the core features that will be built in the initial phase (MVP).
    *   For each feature, briefly describe what it does from a user's perspective.
    *   *Optional:* Include user stories (e.g., "As a [type of user], I want to [action], so that [benefit]").
*   **User Experience (UX) Considerations:** (Any specific design principles, key user flows, or UI requirements)
*   **Technical Considerations / High-Level Architecture:** (Briefly mention any significant technical requirements or constraints, e.g., "API integration required with X," "must support mobile web")

---

### **6. Out of Scope**

*   **What are we explicitly NOT doing in this phase?** (Helps manage expectations and prevent scope creep)

---

### **7. Success Metrics & KPIs**

*   **How will we track performance?** (Reiterate and expand on the metrics from Section 3)
    *   *Example:* User adoption rate (daily/weekly active users), feature usage frequency, conversion rates, customer satisfaction (CSAT/NPS), support ticket volume.
*   **Baseline:** What are the current numbers for these metrics (if available)?

---

### **8. Dependencies & Risks**

*   **Key Dependencies:**
    *   What internal teams, external partners, or existing systems are we reliant on?
    *   What information or resources are crucial for this project's success?
*   **Potential Risks:**
    *   What could go wrong? (Technical challenges, market changes, resource constraints, adoption issues)
    *   What are our mitigation strategies for these risks?

---

### **9. Timeline (High-Level)**

*   **Key Milestones:** (e.g., Design Complete, Development Start, Alpha/Beta Testing, Target Launch Date)
*   **Expected Duration:** (e.g., "6-8 weeks for MVP")

---

### **10. Team & Stakeholders**

*   **Core Team:** (Product Manager, Engineering Lead, Design Lead, QA Lead)
*   **Key Stakeholders:** (Executives, Sales, Marketing, Customer Support, Legal)

---

### **11. Open Questions & Future Considerations**

*   **What still needs to be decided or investigated?**
*   **What are potential next steps or future phases beyond this initial scope?**
